Little River Development

Classically, I have been trained in backend development and data manipulation. This is a launchpad for all the ideas that come to me, built with the help of AI tools.

In this day and age there is no barrier to entry when it comes to creating, and Little River Development serves as a portfolio of passion projects that I have built. Thanks for visiting!
